Personal archive

A private tool that keeps its owner's own files in the owner's own Google Drive.

Who uses it

One person: its owner, with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google Drive

The tool signs in with Google's own sign-in page and asks only for drive.file: the files and folders the tool itself creates. It cannot see or change anything else in the Drive. See the privacy policy.
